Plausible Futures Workshop

Name the big uncertainties, pick two, estimate which way each could break, then plan for the four futures they create.

What could go one way or the other?

List the forces that matter to your work but that nobody can predict with confidence. For each one, name what is driving the uncertainty, then describe the two poles: the two opposite ways it could realistically turn out.

Pick the two that matter most

Choose the two uncertainties with the biggest impact on your work that are also the hardest to call. The first you pick becomes the horizontal axis; the second, the vertical.

How likely is each pole?

Drag each slider toward the pole you think is more likely. The two sides of each uncertainty always add up to 100%.

Four plausible futures

Each quadrant is one combination of poles, with its chance of happening. Tap inside a quadrant to name that future and write what you would do if you saw it coming.

Box percentages multiply the two slider values, which assumes the two uncertainties move independently. If they tend to move together, treat the numbers as a rough guide.
